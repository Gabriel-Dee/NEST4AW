The `off_products.json` database snapshot is derived from **Open Food Facts contributors**,
retrieved on 2026-11-14 through the public search API. Individual product source URLs and
ODbL credits are retained in every row. No product photographs are included.

The snapshot is made available under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
Redistribution of this derived database must preserve attribution and applicable share-alike terms.
See [Open Food Facts terms](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use).
This data license does not relicense the application code.

These are community-reported records, not verified USDA records. Null means unknown.
Unit conversions preserve source values; no nutrition values were fabricated to fill gaps.
The demonstration inventory quantities and expiration dates are separately generated synthetic data.
The two named team fixtures in `docs/golden.json` remain explicitly identified as fixtures.
